MICROTONAL NOTE NAMES - CREDITS AND LICENCES
============================================

This file covers the note-name option on the keyboard: the notation itself, the
algorithm that spells it, and the font that draws it. It sits beside the font it
licenses, next to OFL.txt (which covers Kablammo, the title face) - one folder,
one place to look.

The same credits are shown inside the plugin: Menu -> "Note names: credits".


1. THE NOTATION - ups and downs
-------------------------------
Devised by Kite Giedraitis.
    https://en.xen.wiki/w/Kite%27s_ups_and_downs_notation

Ups and downs extends the ordinary letters and sharps/flats with an "up" and a
"down" so that any equal division of the octave can be spelled with the letters
A-G rather than bare step numbers. No code of Kite's is used here; the credit is
for the notation.

In this font the accidentals ride on ordinary ASCII letters: 'c' and 'd' are the
sharp and the double sharp, 'g' and 'h' the three-quarter and the half sharp,
'a' and 'e' the flat and the half flat, 'v' and 'w' a down and an up, '#' a
double down, '\' and '/' a drop and a lift. That is why the names look like
gibberish in any other typeface, and why the keyboard falls back to degree
NUMBERS rather than draw them in one.


4. HELMHOLTZ-ELLIS JI PITCH NOTATION (HEJI) - NOT SHIPPED YET
-------------------------------------------------------------
HEJI, by Marc Sabat and Thomas Nicholson with Wolfgang von Schweinitz
(https://heji.plainsound.org), is the notation this option would use for a
JUST-INTONATION scale, and the reference implementation this port came from
spells it. It is NOT in this build.

The reason is honest and specific: HEJI's accidentals are SMuFL codepoints
(U+E260-E266, U+E2C0-U+E2ED) and the only font here that carries them is a
Bravura subset in WOFF2 form. JUCE's Typeface::createSystemTypefaceFor reads
OTF/TTF, not WOFF2, and there is no OTF/TTF Bravura in this tree. Shipping it
anyway would draw a row of missing-glyph boxes on every JI degree, which is worse
than the degree numbers it replaced. When a Bravura (or other SMuFL) OTF is
added under this folder, hejiName() can be ported the same way edoNames() was.

Bravura, if and when it is added, is Copyright (c) 2019 Steinberg Media
Technologies GmbH, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 - the same licence as
OFL.txt in this folder.
